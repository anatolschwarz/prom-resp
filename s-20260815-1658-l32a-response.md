## Cause

Confirmed mismatch: the physics block is an independently authored, hidden `350 × 30` rectangle at `(380, 280)`, whereas the visible table is baked into the full-scene background and begins around `(308, 361)`. There is no sprite-derived geometry or registration transform tying the two together. The rectangle therefore covers x=205–308 of blank wall (and y=236–324, above the tabletop artwork), which is why the marble contacts it while visibly left of the rendered table.
