The live marble body and display sprite both start at center `(250, 220)`; its Matter circle radius is `18`. Its displayed sprite is `56 × 56` and remains position-aligned with the body.

## 4. Geometric result

The marble is visibly left of the rendered table: its physics-circle right edge is x=`268`, about 40 pixels before the art's left edge at x≈`308`. It is also visually far above the artwork's tabletop (art top y≈`361`).

At tick 0 it is geometrically above the hidden rotated body, not overlapping it:

```text
upper body surface at x=250 = 242.46
perpendicular center-to-surface distance = (242.46 - 220) × cos(0.17) = 22.14
clearance = 22.14 - 18 = 4.14 px
```

After gravity advances the real browser scene, the first collision is on tick 6: `block:tabletop-left-geometry ↔ ball:tea-marble`. Matter reports the marble at `(250.000, 225.737)` immediately before resolution. Its perpendicular distance to the same surface is about `16.48`, so the radius-18 circle has entered the body by about `1.52` pixels. After resolution, the synchronized body/sprite position is `(250.204, 224.551)`.

## 5. Cause

Confirmed: the Matter rectangle occupies visible empty space. Its leftmost world extent is x=`204.985`, 103 pixels left of the rendered table, and its top is y=`235.609`, 125 pixels above the rendered table.

`teaTime.json` independently authors that rectangle at `(380, 280)` with size `350 × 30` and rotation `0.17`. In Storybook presentation, `PrototypeScene.createBlock` hides the block, shadow, and highlight, while `drawWorkshop` renders the table only as part of the full-scene background. No code derives or aligns this collision body from the table pixels. The marble therefore hits the invisible left end of the authored rectangle while its aligned sprite remains in empty artwork space.
