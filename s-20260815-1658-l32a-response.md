# Phase 2 #32 — Tea Time rightward-motion diagnostic

Defect reproduced in the Phaser/Matter browser runtime. No correction was made.

Browser trace, immediately after Run, with no Teapot placed:

| Tick | Position `(x, y)` | Velocity `(vx, vy)` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `(250.000, 235.000)` | `(0.000, 0.000)` |
| 1 | `(251.794, 224.824)` | `(0.019, 0.003)` |
| 4 | `(252.027, 224.849)` | `(0.052, 0.009)` |
| 10 | `(252.679, 224.903)` | `(0.069, 0.002)` |
| 20 | `(254.726, 225.218)` | `(0.177, 0.021)` |

Evidence:

- X increases from the first Matter tick: `250.000 → 251.794`.
- The first and only initial contact is tick 1: `block:tabletop-left-geometry` with `ball:tea-marble`.
- No Teapot exists in the diagnostic run, so no Teapot rule or redirect can be involved.
- The marble is displaced upward from `y=235.000` to `y=224.824` on tick 1, then travels predominantly right: `vx` is consistently much larger than `vy` through tick 20.

Inspection identifies the source: the marble starts at `(250, 235)` with radius 18, already intersecting the top of the fixed, rotated `tabletop-left-geometry` block (`x=380`, `y=280`, `350×30`, rotation `0.17`). Matter resolves that initial penetration on tick 1. Gravity projected along that sloped tabletop creates the rightward velocity. `createBall` does not assign an initial velocity, and no actor, impulse, or redirect is present before that first tabletop contact.

Conclusion: **yes, the browser defect is reproduced.** The marble moves right immediately because of an initial overlap with the sloped tabletop geometry, not because of a Teapot redirect or an explicit initial horizontal velocity.

No gameplay code, geometry, physics behavior, tests, or reference solutions were changed. The diagnostic prompt was appended verbatim to `docs/CODEX_PROMPTS.md`. Contraption Lab was not committed or pushed.
